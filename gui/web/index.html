Compute
at the Edge

One coordinator splits the work. Laptops on the same network pick it up, resize images, and send the results back. No cloud, no server room: just the machines in this room.

Every worker reports its CPU, memory, and battery with a heartbeat every two seconds. The scheduler uses that pulse to decide who gets the next task, and notices when a heart stops.

Live
Workers

Every laptop that joined, as the coordinator sees it right now: idle, busy, unavailable when its CPU, memory, or battery is too low, or failed when it went silent.

Heartbeat

A seismograph for the network. The trace rises with each worker's CPU load, ticks with every heartbeat, and bursts when a task comes back. A flat red line is a worker that stopped answering.

Task

Drop images anywhere on this page. They are queued, shared between the workers, retried on another laptop if one fails, and saved on the coordinator.
